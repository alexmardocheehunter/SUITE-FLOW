## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance spatial computing aesthetic tailored for the next generation of West African enterprise software. It merges hyper-refined glassmorphism, directional optical lighting, and deep pneumatic tactile depth to convey prestige, engineering rigor, and fluid momentum.

### Brand Personality & Core Atmosphere
- **Sovereign & Visionary:** Positioned for market leaders and high-growth enterprises who require world-class tooling rooted in operational precision.
- **Atmospheric & Multi-Dimensional:** UI elements do not sit flatly on a canvas; they occupy calibrated Z-planes illuminated by ambient aurora blooms that react to system activity.
- **Architectural & Sculpted:** Interfaces feel physical, utilizing soft interior extrusions (neumorphic wells and ridges), crisp refraction borders, and deep ambient drop shadows.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Spatial Light Engines:** Surface lighting respects a primary directional overhead light source (120°) combined with contextual ambient underglows keyed to enterprise modules (Electric Blue for sales, Deep Indigo for financial engineering, Emerald for people operations, Royal Purple for legal integrity).
- **Physical Optical Refraction:** Translucent panels are never merely flat opacity fills; they incorporate compound specular borders (1px translucent white gradient rims) and high-density backdrop blurs that separate foreground actions from the dynamic underlying mesh.
- **Dynamic Density:** Dense data matrices effortlessly transition into airy, interactive command cards without visual friction.

## Colors

The system uses a duel-strata palette engineered for high-contrast spatial depth. While primarily rooted in a luminous pearl backdrop, it fluidly incorporates deep midnight carbon layers for analytical focus, executive cockpits, and high-impact bento configurations.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (Pearl Light):** `#F8FAFC` provides the non-glare, pristine physical bedrock across primary operational views.
- **Surface Elevation (Milky Glass):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` to `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.85)` over light zones; `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` to `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.60)` over dark zones.
- **High-Contrast Dark Canvas (Deep Midnight Carbon):** `#0B1120` and `#0F172A` are employed for executive command panels, telemetry modules, and inverted storytelling sequences.
- **Module Identity Accents:**
  - **Electric Blue (Sell Engine / Primary):** `#2563EB` (Base), `#1D4ED8` (Interaction/Active), `#3B82F6` (Luminescent Highlights).
  - **Deep Indigo (Financial & Accounting Flow):** `#4F46E5` (Base), `#4338CA` (Dense Metrics).
  - **Emerald Green (HR, People, & Verification):** `#10B981` (Validated Status), `#059669` (Deep Fill).
  - **Royal Purple (Legal, Compliance, & Protocol):** `#7C3AED` (Base), `#6D28D9` (Elevation Shadow).
  - **Carbon Slate (Task & Workstream Matrix):** `#1E293B` to `#334155`.
  - **Imperial Golden Glow (VIP / Enterprise Yield):** `#F59E0B` to `#D97706` with ambient golden specular reflections.

### Ambient Aurora Radiance
Accent colors must never be confined solely to solid strokes or text fills. They cast soft, low-frequency radial glows (`blur(80px)` to `blur(140px)`) at `12%` opacity behind primary focal planes, simulating dynamic optic lighting inside the spatial UI.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear informational density across analytics, operational flows, and marketing layouts.

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric forms with balanced apertures provide modern, confident momentum. Kerning is deliberately tracked inwards (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on larger scale sizes to maintain tension and impact.
- **Continuous Reading & Body (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible letterforms ensure sustained readability under data-dense enterprise contexts. Features tabular numerals (`tnum`) across all dynamic currency and analytical displays.
- **Telemetry & Technical Tokens (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for enterprise transaction hashes, system statuses, operational logs, and data-density chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a dual system: a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid paired with an autonomous **Spatial Bento System**.

### Spatial Grid Matrix
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `margin-lg: 4rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Maximum canvas constraint is `1440px` for structured operational views, with hero panels breaking out into 100vw light fields.
- **Tablet (768px - 1027px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Bento cards collapse down to 2-column pairings.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm: 1rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Bento panels reflow strictly into dynamic linear stacks.

### Structural Paradigms
- **Sticky Narrative Stacking:** As users scroll through workflow stages, parent narrative panels anchor to the top-left quadrant while secondary diagnostic glass panels slide through depth planes along the right.
- **Modular Bento Grid:** Surfaces are partitioned into discrete informational nodes (1x1, 2x1, 2x2, 3x2) linked visually through shared ambient light gradients and synchronized border refractions.
- **Masonry Flow:** ROI showcases and metric proof-points operate on a Pinterest-style staggered masonry with varying card elevations to prevent cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial visual hierarchy is achieved through a 3D composite stack: backdrop blur, ambient perimeter glow, chromatic refraction borders, and compound pneumatic shadows.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Level 0 (Bedrock Surface):** 
   - Light canvas: `#F8FAFC`
   - Dark command canvas: `#0B1120`
   - No elevation or blur.

2. **Level 1 (Recessed Well / Neumorphic Inset):**
   - Used for data input containers, segmented control tracks, and code telemetry boxes.
   - Light: Inset shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
   - Dark: Inset shadow `inset 0 2px 5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

3. **Level 2 (Glass Plane - Baseline Cards & Bento Nodes):**
   - Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` on light; `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` on dark.
   - Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Optical Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` top/left grading to `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.3)` bottom/right.
   - Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.

4. **Level 3 (Floating Command Surfaces & Active Cards):**
   - Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` on light; `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` on dark.
   - Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(200%)`.
   - Optical Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` on light; `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` on dark.
   - Compound Shadow: Directional shadow + ambient tint: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

5. **Level 4 (Spatial Modal, Hovering Flyout & VIP Tier):**
   - High-impact elevation featuring dynamic aurora backsplashes.
   - Shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
   - Gold VIP Variant: Shadow contains directional amber dispersion: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances humanized softness with corporate architectural precision. Using the `2` (Rounded) geometry scale, the system establishes a uniform structural cadence.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Cards & Bento Modules (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Generously radiused corners create a comfortable, spatial device-like container for complex data.
- **Nested Inner Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Sub-elements sit proportionally inside outer containers without concentric radius distortion.
- **Controls, Input Fields & Micro-Panels (0.5rem / 8px):** Provides sharp functional distinction between display surfaces and interactive touch targets.
- **Interactive Pills, Tags & Floating Actions (`full` / 9999px):** Fully rounded pill geometry is reserved strictly for dynamic filter pills, interactive trigger pills, and verification indicators.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric Blue Glow):** Solid `#2563EB` fill overlaid with an ultra-fine internal gradient (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` at 0% to `transparent` at 100%). Box-shadow: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.39), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `translateY(-1px)` and box shadow expands to `0 6px 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Glass background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`, backdrop blur `12px`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Dark text `#0F172A`. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with subtle pneumatic lift.
- **VIP Enterprise Action:** Gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #F59E0B 0%, #D97706 100%)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, ambient glow `0 4px 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`.

### 2. Interactive Pills & Filter Chips
- Capsule geometry (`rounded-full`) with a height of `32px` or `36px`.
- **Inactive:** Transparent or milky translucent fill `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.7)`, border `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.5)`, text `#475569`.
- **Active:** Matches specific flow accent (e.g., Deep Indigo `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.1)` fill with `#4F46E5` text and `1px solid #4F46E5` border). Features a micro glowing status dot (`6px`) on the leading edge.

### 3. Glass Bento Cards & Nodal Containers
- Multi-layered spatial cards utilizing backdrop blur (`16px`) and multi-stop borders simulating edge refraction.
- Header zones feature high-contrast `label-md` uppercase tags paired with mono metrics.
- Internal divider lines do not use opaque borders; they use subtle gradient fades (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6), transparent)`).

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Recessed neumorphic baseline. Background `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.6)`, inner shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.8)`. Height `44px`, padding `0 1rem`. Focus state: lifts border to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` and background turns solid white.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved squircle borders with micro tactile extrusion. When checked, transitions immediately to `#2563EB` (or relevant module accent) with a high-contrast white check icon and radial halo.

### 5. Tiered Pricing Cards (with Gold VIP)
- **Standard Tiers:** Frosted glass panels with Level 2 elevation, crisp border highlights, and standard primary blue CTAs.
- **VIP Golden Tier:** Elevated to Level 4. Features a dedicated upper ambient light canopy: a radial pseudo-element gradient emitting `#F59E0B` at `15%` opacity. Outlined with a shimmering gold gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FCD34D, #F59E0B, #B45309)`). Accompanied by a badge with a subtle breathing pulse animation.

### 6. Interactive ROI Masonry Cards
- Dynamic Pinterest-style metric cards containing live sparklines, fractional bar gauges, and highlighted ROI percentages.
- Uses subtle 3D tilt responsiveness on hover (`transform: perspective(1000px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...)`) alongside enhanced depth shadows to emphasize tangible value.
